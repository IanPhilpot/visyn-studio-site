We left to fix it.

We met at a major CPG services company. Ian led marketing. Steve ran sales. Phil managed operations. From three different seats, we saw the same problem: growing brands had great products and real momentum, but the agencies around them either ignored them or charged them like Fortune 500 companies.

Then we watched live selling change the game. A founder with a great product and a phone can move more inventory in one stream than a month of static ads. What those founders were missing was everything around the stream: the ads, the follow-up, the tracking, the tools. So that’s what we built Visyn Studio to do.

We don’t do retainers. We get paid when our brands grow, which keeps us honest and keeps us focused on the numbers that matter to you.
