Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

This page explains what visyn.studio collects when you visit, and what we do with it. It’s short because we don’t collect much.

What we collect

When you browse. We use Google Analytics to understand how people use this site: which pages are viewed, how visitors found us, and general details such as device type, browser, and approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart. We see this as aggregate reports, not as information about who you are.

When you get in touch. If you email us or book a growth call, we receive what you send — usually your name, email address, and details about your brand. Calls are booked through Google Calendar’s appointment scheduling, so what you enter there is also handled by Google.

When a page loads. Like most websites, the services that deliver this one receive standard technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to send you the page. GitHub hosts the site, and Google Fonts serves its typefaces.

How we use it

To see what’s working on this site and improve it, to reply when you contact us, and to prepare for calls you book. We don’t sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who else handles it

Google, for analytics, fonts, and call booking, and GitHub, for hosting. Each handles information under its own policy: see how Google uses data from sites that use its services and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on to stop Google Analytics recording your visits. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email hello@visyn.studio. We may need to confirm it’s really you first.

If you’re a client

The data we handle on your behalf — such as your store, ad, and email platform data — is covered by our agreement with you, not by this page.

Changes

If anything here changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

Email hello@visyn.studio. Visyn Studio is based in Chicago.
